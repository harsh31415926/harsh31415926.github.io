About

Most of what I know came from building things that broke first. I'm in my final year of engineering at IET DAVV in Indore, and for the last two years almost all of my free time has gone into machine learning — first the classical stuff, then deep learning, and more recently language models and agents.

What I care about is the whole path, not just the model. Getting features right, picking a baseline before reaching for something clever, understanding why a metric moved, and then actually shipping it so someone can open a link and use it. Four of the projects below are live for exactly that reason.

Right now I'm going deeper on agentic systems and LLM engineering — memory, state, orchestration, evaluation, and the operational side of running these systems reliably. I'm also drawn to quantitative finance, where the modelling problems are unusually honest about being wrong.
